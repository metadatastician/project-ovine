== Why the repo exists

`project-ovine` is a game, but its major role is as a testing ground for the
enaction-engine's *cognitive, affective and conative* AI. That is what `CAC` in
`CACBrainComponent` and `CACSystem` stands for.

The Three.js code in `index.html` and `src/*.js` is a *mockup*, deliberately. It
exists to design the game by trialling how it might look first, to think about
the issues in responses to things, and to work out which behaviours are worth
capturing, modelling, or potentially discarding. A proper implementation in
Rust / a proof language is to be written alongside it. The mockup is meant to
stay cheap to change and cheap to throw away.

== The things to trial

Recorded in the owner's words.

=== Alliance discovered over time, not declared

* "the alien beaming down and trying to release the sheep/cats"
* "them learning that the alien is on their side over time"

=== Confinement as a channel, not just a state

* "the fact the herded are in the pen together having a downside as they get to
  confer and share intelligence on things"

=== The animals themselves

* "the c-a-c characteristics of the animals"
* "idiosyncratic personalities of individuals"

=== Herders you do not directly control

* "later things like having to coordinate your puppy and even train it alongside
  you"
* "things like having more sophisticated intelligences"

=== Role functionality — perception shapes strategy

* "role functionalities so some herders might have prey positioning of eyes
  verses predator focus and how this might affect things"

=== When the usual verb does not work

* "some might be herding predators of themselves, needing to use a lure strategy
  rather than a chase strategy"
* "some might need to coordinate the herding of one animal to herd another
  animal that itself will sort out the herding for you"

=== Prediction as a learned capability

* "the presence of landmines and learning of the ability to predict behaviours
  so you don't herd sheep over them"

=== Later-stage herded and herders

* "ability to build in later stages robotic herders"
* "or genetically modified things you need to herd"

== What is deliberately NOT recorded here

No sequencing, no increments, no acceptance criteria, no data model. Those are
decisions that have not been taken, and writing them here would make taken
decisions out of untaken ones.
