PROJECT OVINE

A hamster, five cats with minds, and an alien gate-hacking problem

◈ Cognitive

Each cat keeps beliefs that decay without perception: where you were, whether the gate is open, whether the pen is a trap — plus a theory of mind of you and a model of what its peers know. Smart cats predict your intercept point.

♥ Affective

Events are appraised into fear, frustration, curiosity, contentment, boredom and trust, summarised as PAD (pleasure / arousal / dominance). Emotion sets speed, flight distance and how beliefs are learned.

➤ Conative

Six homeostatic drives compete. Every tick a utility arbiter picks a goal — flee, raid the gate, huddle, explore, play, rest — with commitment hysteresis so cats don't dither.

Two ways to win: scare cats in with squeaks, or earn their trust by holding still nearby — trusting cats will happily follow you through the gate. Hold all five for 10 seconds. Audio is procedural (Tone.js).
